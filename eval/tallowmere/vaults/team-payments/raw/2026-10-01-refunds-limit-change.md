# Refunds approval limit change

Meeting on 1 October 2026. Present: judy, alice, bob, grace.

## Discussion
- judy reported that support agents were waiting on a second approver for most furniture refunds, because the old threshold of £250 is lower than the price of a typical sofa or dining table.
- grace agreed to raise the threshold. From today, refunds over £500 need a second approver in the admin tool; refunds of £500 or less can be approved by a single support agent.
- The refund window stays at 30 days from delivery.
- Refunds go back to the card through Paystream and normally reach the customer within 5 working days.

## Queue target
- judy wants the refund queue drained within 4 hours during support hours.
- bob said the batch job only runs every 2 hours and 8 hours is the honest target until it is rewritten. Left open.

## Actions
- bob: change the threshold in the admin tool config (done in the meeting).
- judy: tell the support team.
