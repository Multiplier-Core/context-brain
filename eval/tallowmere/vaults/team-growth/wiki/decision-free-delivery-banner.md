# Decision: Keep the old free-delivery banner
The new free-delivery banner lost its Dial test, so the marketing site keeps the old banner.

## Facts
- [result] Experiment `banner-free-delivery-v2` ran from 15 September to 6 October 2026 with 7,300 users per arm #experiment (by: ivan · src: [[raw/2026-10-09-banner-test-readout#Result]] · from: 2026-10-09)
- [result] The new banner lost: conversion was -1.8% against the old banner (p = 0.03) #experiment (by: ivan · src: [[raw/2026-10-09-banner-test-readout#Result]] · from: 2026-10-09)
- [decision] We keep the old banner; a slimmer version will be tried in Q1 2027 #marketing-site (by: erin · src: [[raw/2026-10-09-banner-test-readout#Decision]] · from: 2026-10-09)
