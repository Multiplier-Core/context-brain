# Promo Codes
Rules for promotional codes on the marketing site and at checkout. Owned by the growth team.

## Facts
- [rule] Promo codes can't be stacked with the loyalty discount #stacking (by: ivan · src: [[raw/2026-10-27-growth-slack-digest#Autumn campaign]] · from: 2026-02-01)
- [limit] No promo code may give more than 20% off #discount (by: ivan, erin · src: [[raw/2026-10-27-growth-slack-digest#Autumn campaign]] · from: 2026-02-01)
- [rule] Promo codes expire 30 days after issue unless a campaign sets an earlier end date #expiry (by: erin · from: 2026-02-01)
- [campaign] The autumn campaign code is HEARTH20, 20% off, valid 14 to 30 November 2026 #campaign (by: erin · src: [[raw/2026-10-27-growth-slack-digest#Autumn campaign]] · from: 2026-10-26)
