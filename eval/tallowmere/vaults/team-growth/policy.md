# team-growth

## Purpose
Shared knowledge for the growth team: the marketing site, experiments (Dial), analytics, the newsletter and promotional codes.

## In scope
- Experiment designs, results and decisions taken from them.
- Analytics definitions: how metrics are calculated and where the data comes from.
- Campaign calendars, promo code rules, newsletter conventions.
- Brand and design conventions for the marketing site.

## Always group
- Experiment results and the decisions taken from them.
- Metric definitions the team reports on.
- Promo code rules and campaign dates.

## Out of scope
- Anything personal: health, family, pay, performance, opinions about colleagues.
- Credentials and API keys for analytics or email tools.
- Individual customers' personal data.

## Examples
| Item | Belongs here? |
|---|---|
| "Weekly active buyers counts anyone with a paid order in the last 7 days" | Yes |
| "The free-delivery banner test lost; we keep the old banner" | Yes |
| "Promo codes can't be stacked with the loyalty discount" | Yes |
| "I want to move to the payments team next year" | No (personal) |
| "Frank's mockups were sloppy this week" | No (claim about a person) |
