# Free-delivery banner test readout

Readout on 9 October 2026. Present: erin, frank, ivan.

## Result
- Experiment `banner-free-delivery-v2` ran from 15 September to 6 October, 7,300 users per arm.
- The new free-delivery banner lost: conversion was -1.8% against the old banner (p = 0.03).
- frank's view is that the new banner pushed the search box below the fold on mobile.

## Decision
- We keep the old banner. frank will try a slimmer version in Q1 2027.
