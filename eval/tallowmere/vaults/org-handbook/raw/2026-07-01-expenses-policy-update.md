# Expenses policy update

Announcement from heidi, 1 July 2026.

## What changes
- Expense claims must now be submitted within 60 days of the purchase. The old limit was 90 days.
- Claims over £250 still need a manager's approval before they are paid.
- The daily meal allowance on work trips stays at £35.

## Why
Finance closes each quarter faster now, and claims older than 60 days were holding up the close.
