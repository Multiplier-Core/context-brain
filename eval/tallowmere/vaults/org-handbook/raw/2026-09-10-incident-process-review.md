# Incident process review

Meeting on 10 September 2026. Present: grace, heidi, carol, alice.

## Severity
- SEV1 means customers can't check out. SEV2 means a major feature is degraded but checkout works. SEV3 is everything else that needs a response.
- For a SEV1, an incident commander must be named within 15 minutes of the first page.

## Postmortems
- grace wants every SEV1 and SEV2 postmortem published within 5 working days, while memories are fresh.
- heidi pointed out that 5 days clashes with people being off after a long incident and proposed 10 working days. The handbook records both until the next review.
- Postmortems are blameless; they never name a person as the cause.
