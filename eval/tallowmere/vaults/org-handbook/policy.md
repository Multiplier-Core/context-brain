# org-handbook

## Purpose
How Tallowmere works as a company: policies on leave, expenses, travel, security and incidents, and company dates. Everyone can read it; only grace and heidi can write to it.

## In scope
- Company policies and changes to them.
- Company-wide dates: holidays, office closures, all-hands.
- The incident process and severity definitions.
- Security policy that applies to everyone.

## Always group
- Policy changes announced by people operations or the head of engineering.

## Out of scope
- How an individual uses a policy ("I'm taking parental leave in March").
- Team-specific technical knowledge; that belongs to the team's group.
- Pay, performance or HR cases about named people.

## Examples
| Item | Belongs here? |
|---|---|
| "Expense claims over £250 need a manager's approval" | Yes |
| "The office is closed from 24 December to 2 January" | Yes |
| "SEV1 means customers can't check out" | Yes |
| "I'm claiming the train to the offsite" | No (personal) |
| "Heidi is handling Bob's grievance" | No (HR matter about a person) |
