# Leave
Annual leave and how to book it.

## Facts
- [entitlement] Everyone gets 27 days of annual leave plus UK bank holidays #annual-leave (by: heidi · from: 2025-01-01)
- [rule] Up to 5 unused days can be carried over, and must be used by 31 March #carry-over (by: heidi · from: 2025-01-01)
- [rule] The office closure from 24 December to 2 January doesn't count against annual leave #holidays (by: heidi · src: [[raw/2026-10-15-all-hands-notes#Holidays]] · from: 2026-10-15)
- [process] Leave is booked in the HR tool at least 2 weeks ahead for anything longer than 3 days #booking (by: heidi · from: 2025-01-01)
