# Incident Process
How Tallowmere declares, runs and learns from incidents. Applies to every team.

## Facts
- [definition] SEV1 means customers can't check out #severity (by: grace · src: [[raw/2026-09-10-incident-process-review#Severity]] · from: 2026-09-10)
- [definition] SEV2 means a major feature is degraded but checkout works; SEV3 is everything else that needs a response #severity (by: grace · src: [[raw/2026-09-10-incident-process-review#Severity]] · from: 2026-09-10)
- [rule] For a SEV1, an incident commander must be named within 15 minutes of the first page #roles (by: grace · src: [[raw/2026-09-10-incident-process-review#Severity]] · from: 2026-09-10)
- [rule] Postmortems are blameless and never name a person as the cause #postmortems (by: grace, heidi · src: [[raw/2026-09-10-incident-process-review#Postmortems]] · from: 2026-09-10)
